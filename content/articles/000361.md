### Visualizing Labor

Starosielski plumbs the history of the cable network, offering us a view of the
spaces through which our signal traffic passes haunted by a colonial past.
Unearthing the cables means uncovering the lives of the workers who laid and
maintained them, and the islanders whose land the cables passed through. She
exposes the cables as an agent of colonial modernization, documenting the
portrayal of native populations and the body performances of becoming
